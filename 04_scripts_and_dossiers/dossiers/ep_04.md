# Episode 04 Dossier: Why AI Erased the First Career Rung

## 1. Structural Thesis and 2027-to-2036 Horizon Immunity

Corporate AI adoption is severing the bottom rung of white-collar employment while leaving senior headcount flat across administrative payroll records. Multi-agent coding, contract-review, and support swarms absorb junior ticket triage and boilerplate drafting inside `$1,500` monthly per-seat enterprise compute caps (`FL-032`), keeping experienced human reviewers in place to verify final output. Cutting `16%` to `19%` of entry roles for workers aged twenty-two to twenty-five (`FL-061`) lifts quarterly operating margins today while starving two long-horizon ledgers: the Social Security FICA entry base and the seasoned mid-level operator bench that corporations will need between 2032 and 2036.

This briefing holds full horizon immunity across 2027 through 2036 because the fault line sits inside multi-year human apprenticeship physics. Senior staff architects, litigation leads, and underwriting directors take five to seven years of supervised error correction to build. Every graduate class locked out in 2027 or 2028 becomes a missing cohort of thirty-year-old team leads by 2033, turning campus hiring freezes and quarterly labor-share prints (`FL-016`) into empirical confirmation of the thesis.
