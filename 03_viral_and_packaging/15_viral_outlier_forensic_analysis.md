### 1. Asymmetric unit-cost and speed collision (`ARCH-OUT-01`)

`ARCH-OUT-01` places a small, fast autonomous input right next to a slow, multi-billion-dollar legacy asset inside a single clause. Viewers do the division in their heads before clicking. In `OUT-001` (`How $20,000 Drones Break $2B Warships`, `37` chars, `21.4x`), the title pits the cheap munition against the hull while the blueprint thumbnail supplies the missing middle number (`$4M SHOT` per naval interceptor). That three-number circuit drives `ep_19` directly.

Temporal mismatches obey the identical arithmetic law. `OUT-002` (`15-Minute AI Exploits vs 43-Day Patches`, `39` chars, `19.8x`) contrasts UIUC's LLM exploit synthesis clock against Verizon DBIR's median enterprise patching lag for `ep_09`, while `OUT-003` (`Why 6-Month AI Hits a 128-Week Grid Queue`, `41` chars, `11.6x`) pits software release cadence against grain-oriented electrical steel transformer lead times for `ep_08`. Whenever *The Reading Room* runs `STR-MAX-ESSAY` or `STR-AOTP-LADDER`, leading with the unit-cost ratio beats naming the industry first.

### 2. Forensic footnote and statutory loophole expose (`ARCH-OUT-02`)

Financial audiences distrust melodrama, yet they click hard on specific accounting mechanics that quietly shift losses onto passive households. `OUT-010` (`Big Tech's $176B Server Depreciation Trick`, `42` chars, `15.4x`) works because the thumbnail crops an authentic SEC Form 10-K useful-life schedule marked `NOTE 1` in brick-red `#A3402E` ink alongside a calm presenter in shirt and tie (`ep_03`).

Who Steps In Now?`, `47` chars, `15.9x`) applies the identical balance-sheet deadlock to `ep_02` as `4% YIELD` domestic JGBs pull `$1.07T` of Japanese duration demand home.

### 4. Single-point physical chokepoint (`ARCH-OUT-04`)

Cold Browse audiences assume software lives in weightless servers. ## Mobile truncation mechanics and pixel budgets

Across `cohort_projection_jan2028.json`, `Browse Features` (`49.60%` of impressions) and `Suggested` (`40.10%` of impressions) generate `89.70%` of total channel impressions, with over `70%` rendered on mobile viewports (`210x118 px` compact cards up to `360x202 px` home feed slots) or narrow `168x94 px` Suggested rails. Every title and thumbnail in *The Reading Room* compiles against four geometric constraints:

1. Character envelopes across surfaces: Across all `54` outliers in `viral_outliers_db.json`, title lengths sit strictly between `37` and `50` characters (mean `45.2` characters). Mobile Home Browse wraps titles onto two lines and truncates with an ellipsis around `48` to `54` characters depending on proportional glyph width (`$` and `W` consume `1.6x` the horizontal width of `i` or `1`), while Suggested `Up Next` rails truncate as early as `38` to `44` characters. Every Reading Room title caps hard at `55` characters, targets `40` to `49` characters, and places the primary dollar figure, statutory noun, or unit-cost collision inside characters `1` through `36`.
2. Zero-to-three word thumbnail ceiling: In `viral_outliers_db.json`, `3` outliers use `0` words (`OUT-029, 036, 042`), `5` use `1` word, `43` use `2` words, and `3` use `3` words (`0` entries exceed `3` words). At `168x94 px`, four or more overlay words collapse below `9 px` cap height and fail a half-second squint test. Overlay text renders exclusively in `Newsreader` (editorial stamps) or `IBM Plex Mono` (numeric deltas and docket codes) at `>= 92 pt` on a `1920x1080` master canvas.
3. Bottom-right timestamp occlusion dead zone: YouTube overlays an opaque runtime badge (`24:30` to `35:00`) over the bottom-right corner (`x: 1520..1895, y: 875..1055` on a `1920x1080` frame, plus full-width red progress bars along `y: 1056..1080` for returning viewers). No word, chart inflection point, statutory stamp, or presenter hand gesture may enter `x >= 1500, y >= 860`.
4. Two-word uppercase cap in titles: Capitalizing entire clauses reads as tabloid spam to high-RPM finance and engineering audiences (`$25.50` to `$33.20` sovereign-finance RPM). Limit uppercase tokens in titles to genuine institutional initialisms (`AI`, `US`, `SEC`, `ASML`, `NIST`, `PJM`, `FICA`, `EUV`, `SVB`).

## Six Browse CTR anti-patterns banned from packaging

In `growth_simulation.py`, any upload whose Browse CTR stalls below `4.70%` gets trapped inside Layer 1 (`Seed Cohort`, `85K` to `195K` base impressions), missing Layer 2 (`>= 4.70%`), Layer 3 (`>= 5.30%`, `W >= 0.62`), and Layer 4 (`>= 6.00%`, `W >= 0.74`). Auditing underperforming uploads across the reference creator channels isolates six packaging mistakes that systematically depress Browse CTR below `4.5%`:

1. Verbal echo across surfaces: Writing `"Big Tech's $176B Profit Trick"` in the title and stamping `"$176B PROFIT TRICK"` on the thumbnail wastes half the pixel budget saying one fact twice. Enforce strict lexical disjunction (`package_lint.py` in Deliverable 17): zero shared content words or identical numbers between title and thumbnail overlay.
2. Uncropped spreadsheet soup: Pasting a raw `14-column` FRED, SEC EDGAR, or Bloomberg screenshot onto a thumbnail turns into gray static at `210x118 px`. Always rebuild or crop down to a single row, two bars, or four words of magnified statutory print on `#FAF8F3` or `#F4EFE6` stock.
3. Academic syllabus or episode-number prefixes: Opening a title with `"Episode 08:"`, `"The Economics of..."`, or `"How Data Center Permitting Works"` signals homework and pushes mobile truncation past the `42`-character cliff before any tension arrives. Keep episode numbers exclusively inside `episodes-vault.json` and playlist metadata.
4. Premature answer spoilers in the package: If `ep_01`'s thumbnail listed `"UBI Vetoed, Compute Scrip Illegal, Grid Locked"`, the viewer would have the thesis verdict without clicking (`R02`/`A16` violation). Package the balance-sheet trap (`86% WAGES` vs `$40.5T Debt`), and let Acts I through III test the escape hatches on screen.
5. Retail hype-face and neon-arrow dissonance: Neon `#00FF00` / `#FF0000` arrows, artificial flames, and shocked facial expressions repel institutional, engineering, and household savers while luring low-intent clickers who abandon the video before `0:30` (`< 58%` retention), destroying `W = CTR_browse * Duration * AVD`. Stick strictly to `PAGE_PALETTE` (`#1C2B3A`, `#A3402E`, `#2F8F8B`) and `OVER_FOOTAGE_PALETTE` tokens.
6. Unvalidated 15-second bait-and-switch: Promising `"15-Minute AI Exploits"` in the package and opening with a two-minute historical biography of the internet triggers an immediate `28%` opening retention cliff. Every outlier entry in `viral_outliers_db.json` carries a binding `first_15s_validation_rule` requiring the exact promised artifact, dollar figure, or clock comparison to appear on screen inside `0:00` to `0:15` (`<= 50` spoken words).
