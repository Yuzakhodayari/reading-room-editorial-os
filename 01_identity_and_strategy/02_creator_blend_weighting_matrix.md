A pure populist money explainer loses institutional credibility when it caricatures central bankers or skips `10-K` footnotes. ### Individual Creator DNA Profiles

#### 1. AI In Context (`7.0x` Top Anchor, Aric Floyd)
Floyd sets the upper ceiling for editorial composure. When a topic involves existential technological shifts or institutional breakdown, amateur channels raise their vocal pitch and stack alarming adjectives. Floyd lowers the temperature. Scenes open with verified calendar dates, named actors, and physical rooms (`T02`). Fear gets punctured with a single dry parenthetical aside placed right after the documented fact (`L23`), then the script returns immediately to primary evidence. On `STR-AIC-CLOCKED` and `STR-AIC-SPECULATIVE` runs, mid-roll sponsors disappear entirely so documentary momentum stays unbroken from minute one to the final civic ask.

#### 2. Max Fisher (`6.0x` Top Anchor)
Fisher supplies the physical investigation table. Every claim rests on a showable primary record: Monthly Treasury Statement `Table 9`, a Federal Reserve wealth survey, a wholesale grid auction clearing sheet, or a Supreme Court ruling (`R30`, `R47`). Once the opening paradox lands, Fisher organizes messy macro systems into three or four numbered investigation bays (`R12`) and checks in with the viewer (`R16`: `"Okay, with me so far?"`) after dense plumbing turns. Most important is Fisher's rule on adversaries (`R19`, `R40`). Foreign central banks, corporate treasurers, and grid operators act on rational balance-sheet incentives. Showing why their choices make sense inside their own constraints makes the systemic trap ten times scarier than a partisan rant.

#### 3. Gen (`5.0x` Own-Channel Anchor, Shirt-and-Tie Restraint)
Gen is the presenter's native bloodstream, recalibrated for a studio reading room. The presenter sits on camera at age eighteen, looking ahead to age twenty-eight in `2036`, dressed in shirt-and-tie restraint. Every trace of casual YouTuber slop stays outside the door: zero profanity (`L13` locked at `0`), zero youth slang (`L14` locked at `0`), zero street-interview padding (`R46`). What survives at full `5.0x` intensity is Gen's refusal to leave macroeconomics in the clouds. Every fiscal or AI curve gets dragged down to Friday payroll withholding, a `$3,400` fixed thirty-year mortgage draft, an October heating bill, or an Austin air-conditioning invoice (`R03`, `T35`). Gen also drives active objection pre-emption (`L17`), calling out the exact counter-argument forming in the viewer's head before skeptics can type a comment, and closes with a concrete ownership lever (`R26`).

#### 4. Maxinomics (`4.5x` Anchor)
Maxinomics contributes visual and syntactic compression. In the `76`-video training corpus, Maxinomics posts the shortest mean sentence length (`12.98` words) and the highest share of short punch lines (`30.53%` at six words or fewer), acting as the counterweight that keeps Fisher's analytical clauses and Gen's spoken momentum from bloating past `17.5` words. Conceptually, Maxinomics turns abstract ratios into two physical objects colliding in frame: a `$20,000` drone meeting a `$4,000,000` interceptor, or a `$120` pair of running shoes stripped down to stitched hours, Memphis freight, and electricity (`STR-MAX-ESSAY`).

#### 5. Art of the Problem (`4.0x` Anchor)
Art of the Problem governs how technical mechanisms get taught from the ground floor (`T23`). Whenever an episode introduces debt deflation, wholesale megawatt-day capacity auctions, or token depreciation curves, AotP forbids dropping undefined jargon onto the screen. You build a small, concrete model with named parts and small numbers first, often anchored by a dated historical precedent (`1912` West Virginia coal scrip, `1933` gold-standard debt freezes, `1941` British ration books), and add one rung of institutional complexity at a time until the modern crisis clicks into place.

#### 6. How Money Works (`2.5x` Supporting)
How Money Works enters whenever financial or statutory plumbing has to be explained cleanly before its failure mode makes sense (`STR-HMW-PRIMER`). HMW provides the polite, self-aware prep line (`L19`) before a dense mechanism block and owns the classic midpoint hinge (`0.46` to `0.54`): first establish how tax withholding, BDC covenants, or collateralized loans work on a normal Tuesday, then pull out the single load pin that AI capital expenditure just snapped.

#### 7. Patrick Boyle (`2.5x` Supporting)
Boyle is the resident forensic accountant. Whenever a press release celebrates record revenue or trillions in capital spending, Boyle opens the cash-flow statement and notes, in a flat deadpan cadence, that back-to-back quarters of negative free cash flow or `100%` bonus depreciation write-offs tell a very different story (`STR-BOYLE-CHAPTERED`, `L26`). Jokes never announce themselves. Stating an absurd balance-sheet gap in plain accounting English is the punchline.

## 2. Axis 1 Breakdown: Structure (`channel-structure`)

Structure governs runtime geometry, engine selection, suspense discipline, and commercial boundaries before a single paragraph of narration gets drafted. Every script begins by emitting a `STRATEGY PACKET` (`R41`, `R42`) with one canonical `STR-*` preset and at least one explicitly banned narrative engine (`R43`).

### Opening Clock and Suspense Preservation (`R01`, `R02`, `A16`)
1. **The `<50`-Word Cold-Open Clock:** While the broader corpus fails hooks past `60` words (`R01`), The Reading Room enforces a stricter house clock: the first concrete financial, physical, or institutional stake must land inside **`<50` words** (roughly `18` seconds at `150` spoken words per minute). Throat-clearing definitions and `"In this video we will cover"` agendas fail on sight.
2. **Thesis Question Hard Cap (`R02`):** The central question driving the episode must stand in plain spoken English by **`0.08`** of runtime (`0.05` on `STR-AIC-CLOCKED`, `0.06` on `STR-AOTP-LADDER`, `0.07` on `STR-GEN-FIELD`, `0.09` on `STR-MAX-ESSAY`). Title claims restate inside the first `10%` and return transformed in the final `10%`.
3. **Unspoiled Escape-Hatch & Board Architecture (`R02` + `A16`):** Introducing a three-part board in the opening `10%` names the candidate fixes or suspects without giving away the mechanical reason each one breaks. In Episode 01, naming a federal check, Universal Basic Compute, and central-bank money printing sets up three open loops (`A16`), while revealing *why* Alaska's Supreme Court, airline-seat token spoilage, and PJM's `$269.92` megawatt-day bottleneck break them belongs strictly inside their dedicated middle acts. Every open loop closes within `40%` of runtime (`A16`) and before `0.90` (`T45`).

## 3. Axis 2 Breakdown: Storytelling (`channel-storytelling`)

Storytelling converts structural bays into human consequence and physical intuition while obeying every `forbidden_engines` lock in the incoming packet (`T43`).

### Longitudinal Character Vehicles (`T09`, `T14`, `T35`, `T39`)
Macroeconomic papers fail on YouTube when households appear only as aggregate deciles. Every Reading Room script anchors its mechanism inside one named or clearly situated human balance sheet introduced within the first `15%` to `20%` of runtime and carried across the full timeline (`T44` single-POV spine). In Episode 01, Claire (`32`-year-old Austin software engineer locking in a `$3,400` fixed thirty-year mortgage in late `2025`) is our longitudinal stress-test instrument:
- In Act I (`0.15` to `0.30`), her `70%` pay cut against a frozen `$3,400` monthly draft proves Irving Fisher's `1933` debt-deflation trap even when the Federal Reserve prevents economy-wide price deflation.
- In Act II (`0.45` to `0.60`), her `$45,000` `401(k)` balance refutes the Wall Street index-fund rebuttal in two sentences, and her monthly rent exposes the debt-ceiling vulnerability of a statutory federal dividend.
- In the Close (`0.90` to `0.96`), returning to Claire in Austin in October `2036` (`T39` bookend callback) demonstrates how monetary dilution shrinks her real mortgage burden while pricing her out of August air conditioning.

### Tactile Physical Scale Ladders (`T19`, `T23`, `L29`)
Viewers cannot picture `945` terawatt-hours, `$2.1` trillion in net Treasury interest, or `99%` token price deflation as raw digits. Every major number above `1,000` triggers a tactile rescaling comparison within two sentences (`L29`), and every physical bottleneck climbs at least a three-rung ladder (`T19`):
- **Rung 1 (Hand-scale object):** A bedside phone charger drawing `10` watts, or an empty airline seat vanishing the second the plane pushes back from the gate.
- **Rung 2 (Room-scale appliance):** A kitchen microwave pulling `1,000` watts (`1` kilowatt), or a six-day expiring digital voucher in Shenzhen.
- **Rung 3 (City-scale aggregation):** One million microwaves running day and night to hit `1` gigawatt, equal to the steady residential load of a major American city.
- **Rung 4 (Institutional shock):** A single `5`-gigawatt AI campus demanding five cities' worth of household electricity from one regional utility.

### Adversary and Counterparty Logic (`R07`, `R19`, `R40`)
Cartoon villains destroy analytical authority. Combining Fisher (`6.0x`), Boyle (`2.5x`), and HMW (`2.5x`), The Reading Room requires two distinct fairness beats in argumentative scripts:
1. **Early Steelman (`<0.25` to `0.30`, `R07`):** Credit what engineers, markets, or founders get right before attacking their conclusion (for example, showing why swapping `80%` of human labor hours in a `$120` running shoe drives unit manufacturing costs down toward electricity).
2. **Primary Structural Concession (`0.60` to `0.85`, `R19`):** Dedicate a fair counter-section to the strongest orthodox defense: Fed fiat inflation targeting vs `1933` gold constraints, GAAP legality of `100%` hardware expensing, or `10`-to-`15`-year grid turbine supply responses. Show why smart people built the rule, then prove the exact timing or accounting seam where the rule still breaks.

### Decile Re-Hook Law and Comment Triggers (`R13`, `A23`)
Every script divides into `10` equal word-count deciles. Under `SHARED_DECILE_REHOOK_POLICY`, every single decile must contain at least **`1`** verified retention reset from the seven approved mechanisms: `contradiction-question`, `primary-artifact-open`, `numeric-shock`, `burstiness-slam`, `entity-callback`, `counterfactual-turn`, or `scale-ladder-step`. When prompting comments in the final decile (`A23`), generic filler (`"Let me know your thoughts below"`) is banned. Comment triggers pose a sharp, contested institutional trade-off that informed viewers want to debate using real evidence.

### Five Spoken-Cadence Engineering Laws
1. **Burstiness Without Parataxis (`L01`, `L51`):** After a `24`-to-`29`-word analytical build carrying a balance-sheet surprise, drop a `4`-to-`8`-word declarative punch (`"All three of them break."` / `"See how far it gets you."`). Never stack three short fragments in a row, and never allow three consecutive sentences of identical word count.
2. **Talk Through Contractions and Coordinating Starters (`L02`, `L09`):** Contract natural spoken auxiliaries (`don't`, `won't`, `doesn't`, `she's`, `money's`), reserving uncontracted forms (`"It is not a routine auction"`) strictly for deliberate vocal stress. Start clauses with `And`, `So`, or `But` where academic prose reaches for stiff Latinate connectors.
3. **Say Every Structural Turn Out Loud (`L16`):** Never pivot silently between paragraphs. Signal the hinge in plain speech (`"Problem is, that whiteboard's never met a thirty-year loan."` / `"Now let's be fair to the accounting."` / `"So why does it fall apart?"`), varying the turn phrasing across every act break.
4. **Human Epistemic Hedges Over Lawyer Padding (`L36`):** Ban throat-clearing disclaimers. When timing or political path is uncertain, speak in first person (`"None of this is financial advice, and I don't know the exact month each curve bends."`).
5. **Absolute Punctuation and Lexical Cleanliness (`L31`, `L32`, `L33`, `L50`):** Zero semicolons in spoken scripts, zero em dashes or double-hyphen splices, zero exclamation marks, zero negative-contrast pivot crutches, zero abstract noun triads, and zero hits from the banned AI vocabulary catalog.

## 8. Four-Axis Audit Arithmetic and Ownership Boundaries

### Deterministic `0` to `10` Scoring Formula
Each axis begins at **`10.0`** and applies unsoftened arithmetic deductions:
- **`-2.0` points** per missing or broken `P0` card that applies to the script. Any single `P0` flag caps that axis at **`6.0 / 10`** and blocks publication outright.
