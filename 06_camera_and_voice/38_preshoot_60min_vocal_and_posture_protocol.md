## Phase 1 (`T-60:00` to `T-50:00`): Room Silence, Thermal Settling, and Hydration

Turn off all overhead room ceiling fixtures immediately upon entering the study. Overhead fixtures cast harsh sockets under the brow and flatten the walnut shelving behind the desk. Bring up only the `4600K` camera-left key softbox, the `5000K` rim light, and the `2700K` to `3000K` practical desk lamp so studio equipment stabilizes thermally before white-balance verification.

Silence the acoustic floor down to `-58 dBFS` or lower with room decay (`RT60`) kept inside `0.22` to `0.32` seconds. Power down noisy desktop fans, air conditioners, and nearby kitchen appliances, and switch phones and smartwatches to airplane mode. Clean silence matters directly to `prep-aroll.mjs`, which slices dead pauses below `-34.0 dB` lasting longer than `0.65` seconds.

Drink `350 ml` of plain room-temperature water slowly across these ten minutes, saving the remaining `350 ml` beside the desk out of frame. Leave milk, yogurt, sugary tea, ice water, and heavy coffee outside the room before A-roll, because dairy thickens mucus on vocal cords while cold water tightens laryngeal muscles. If morning throat dryness lingers, inhale warm plain water vapor for three minutes before speaking a single loud word.

## Phase 2 (`T-50:00` to `T-42:00`): Shirt Collar, Tie Dimple, and Wardrobe Check

Dress directly under studio key light so fabric behavior matches the `35mm` sensor. Wear a `140` to `170 gsm` Oxford cloth button-down (`OCBD`) or matte brushed cotton poplin in warm unbleached bone, ecru, or parchment (`#F2ECE1` to `#E6DFD1`), switching to muted slate-chambray (`#D6E0EA`) only for engineering or grid episodes. Avoid optical-brightener white shirts (`#FFFFFF`), which blow past `88 IRE` at the collarbone, and reject micro-gingham, tight herringbone, or narrow pinstripes under `2.0 mm` weave pitch that cause Bayer color moire and break MediaPipe person matting.

Fasten the top collar button and slide one index finger horizontally between the cotton band and your neck. That single-finger margin prevents jugular compression and lets the larynx drop freely on low-pitched emphasis without buckling the collar points.

Tie a `7.5` to `8.0 cm` matte grenadine silk, brushed wool challis, or raw slub shantung blade in a classic four-in-hand knot matched to the active episode color (`#1C2B3A` Prussian navy for statutory episodes, `#8C1D18` or `#A3402E` oxblood/brick-red for debt episodes, `#1B4D3E` dark forest for solvent cash-flow episodes, `#234161` slate wool for grid and hardware episodes). Before cinching the knot snug against the collar gap, pinch a centered fold with your index finger right below the base of the knot to form one crisp dimple, keeping the knot symmetrical and the front blade anchored squarely at belt height. Fasten barrel cuffs cleanly for Act I, saving any neat two-turn below-elbow sleeve roll for planned ledger breakdowns. Dust a light matte translucent powder across the forehead, nose bridge, and upper cheeks to control key-light shine.

## Phase 3 (`T-42:00` to `T-32:00`): Diaphragm Breathing, Resonance, and Jaw Release

### Minutes 1 to 4 (`T-42:00` to `T-38:00`): Low Diaphragm Rib Expansion

Stand upright with one palm flat across your sternum and the other palm across your lower ribs just above the navel. Inhale silently through your nose over `4` seconds while expanding your lower ribs outward to the sides, keeping your chest palm and collarbones still. High shoulder breathing pulls collar fabric upward into the boom mic and introduces noisy gasps before teleprompter turns. Exhale steadily on a clean, unvoiced `/s/` hiss for `12` seconds on round one, `16` seconds on round two, and `20` seconds on round three, keeping air pressure completely level until empty.

### Minutes 5 to 7 (`T-38:00` to `T-35:00`): Resonance Placement and Chest Grounding

Run three sets of relaxed `20`-second lip trills (blowing gently through closed loose lips like a quiet engine) gliding upward five notes and smoothly back down to your comfortable lower speaking third. Follow with slow `"mmm-mah"` and `"ng-ah"` hums while sensing steady vibration on the front roof of the mouth and upper incisors. Placing vocal resonance forward into the hard palate while staying anchored in your natural chest register gives an eighteen-year-old voice steady authority across twenty-five-minute financial breakdowns without forcing artificial low vocal fry.

### Minutes 8 to 10 (`T-35:00` to `T-32:00`): Jaw Hinge, Tongue, and Plosive Release

Unclench your molars so two stacked fingers fit loosely between front teeth, then massage both jaw hinge muscles (`masseters`) with circular fingertips for `30` seconds. Trace ten slow circles inside your lips with your tongue tip in each direction to relax tongue-root tension, which otherwise pulls back-of-mouth Persian vowels too far into the throat. Finish with eight crisp bursts of `"pa-ta-ka / pa-ta-ka / pa-ta-ka"` keeping lips, tongue tip, and soft palate light and springy.

## Phase 4 (`T-32:00` to `T-24:00`): Dedicated 8-Minute English-from-Persian Articulation Drill

Execute this exact eight-minute drill sitting upright at the desk in full wardrobe under studio light so muscle memory matches actual shooting posture. Read every line aloud once slowly for physical placement, then once cleanly at `150 WPM`.

### Block 4A (`T-32:00` to `T-29:30`, 2.5 Minutes): `/w/` Versus `/v/` Minimal Pairs

Persian merges `/w/` and `/v/` under one written letter. Round both lips forward into an `oo` ring with zero tooth contact on `/w/`, then bring upper incisors cleanly onto the lower lip on `/v/`. Speak each pair aloud across three deliberate passes, holding the contrast sharp:

2. `wine / vine`
3. `worse / verse`
4. `whale / veil`
5. `wall / vault`
6. `wire / via`

10. `West Virginia / Silicon Valley`
11. `CoreWeave / Fedwire`
12. `Wall Street / water / value / revenue`

### Block 4B (`T-29:30` to `T-26:30`, 3.0 Minutes): Initial `sC-` Cluster Cold-Start Drills

Persian syllable structure rejects two starting consonants, tempting speakers to slip an extra `e-` vowel before `st-`, `sp-`, `sk-`, `sm-`, or `str-`. Hold a pure unvoiced snake hiss (`/s/`) for one full second before snapping into the second consonant with zero throat buzz prior to release. Pause two completely silent seconds before each word so cold starts mimic post-edit sentence cuts:

1. `[2s silence] scrip` (start purely on `/s/`, never `e-scrip`)
2. `[2s silence] stablecoin` (start purely on `/s/`, never `e-stablecoin`)
3. `[2s silence] spread` (triple `/spr/` onset)
4. `[2s silence] spreadsheet`
5. `[2s silence] statement`
6. `[2s silence] statute`
7. `[2s silence] spending`

### Block 4C (`T-26:30` to `T-24:00`, 2.5 Minutes): Dental Fricatives and Number-Cluster Clarity

Place the tongue tip gently between upper and lower front teeth for unvoiced `/θ/` (`three`, `thousand`, `thirty`, `months`) and voiced `/ð/` (`Tether`, `withholding`, `smoothed`), keeping `/s/` and `/z/` substitutions out of your read. ## Phase 5 (`T-24:00` to `T-16:00`): Camera Optics, Lighting, and Desk Prop Audit

Confirm camera settings against the `reading-room-cinema` contract before sitting down for framing: `35mm` full-frame focal equivalent (`23` to `24mm` APS-C), `f/2.8` to `f/4.0` aperture, `118` to `122 cm` seated optical axis height at `0 deg` tilt, `30.00 fps` CFR, `1/60s` shutter (`180 deg`), native ISO (`400` or `800`), and 10-bit 4:2:2 flat or log profile.

Check false color or waveform exposure across your key and fill zones: lit cheek and forehead at `58` to `62 IRE` under the `4600K` `45 deg` octabox with eggcrate grid, shadow cheek at `32` to `36 IRE` beside the `120x180 cm` Duvetyne flag (`4:1` ratio), practical warm lamp shade kept strictly below `78 IRE`, and walnut shelves sitting comfortably inside `18` to `24 IRE` separated by the `5000K` rear-left rim.

Inspect physical props on the `60x90 cm` matte dark leather desk blotter (which absorbs chin bounce and eliminates `220` to `280 Hz` early desk reflections). Lay out uncoated cream cotton primary filings (`100` to `120 gsm`, `#F4EFE6`), pre-marked with chisel-tip warm amber marker sweeps and brick-red (`#A3402E`) margin brackets, alongside the uncapped matte black or brass fountain pen angled diagonally and the active episode's foreground scale artifact parked clear of the `rightThird` glass card zone. Keep the work zone uncluttered.

## Phase 6 (`T-16:00` to `T-10:00`): Teleprompter Geometry, Posture, and Hand-Rest Framing

### Teleprompter Distance for Zero Side-to-Side Saccades

Position the beamsplitter teleprompter hood directly in front of the `35mm` lens at `115` to `130 cm` from the bridge of your nose, aligning the vertical center of the text window straight across the optical center of the lens barrel. Restrict the active teleprompter text column to `24` to `28 cm` physical width (`35%` to `42%` of monitor screen width, holding `5` to `6` words per line) at `150 WPM` smooth scroll. Basic trigonometry governs eye sweep: at `120 cm` viewing distance, reading a `26 cm` line subtends `12.3 deg` peak-to-peak (`+/- 6.1 deg` from center), and natural peripheral word recognition keeps physical eye saccades under `3.5 deg` across each line. Wider text boxes push eyeball travel past `12 deg` and create visible side-to-side flickering on `100% -> 112%` numeric punch-in cuts.

### Seated Desk Posture and Frame Zones

Choose a rigid, silent chair with zero creaking springs or swiveling armrests. Sit on the front two-thirds of the seat cushion leaving a `10` to `15 cm` air gap between your lower spine and the chair back, planting both feet flat under knees bent at `90 deg`. Stacking ears over shoulders and shoulders over hips stops lower-back slouching over a thirty-minute shoot and keeps the shirt collar smooth around the neck.

Verify your `1920x1080` grid coordinates on monitor overlay: center at `x = 960px` for `talking-head-full` or shift left-of-center to `x = 680` to `740px` for `talking-head-with-glass`, keeping eyes aligned horizontally across `y = 350` to `375px` and crown clearing `y = 120px` beneath the masthead rule. Leave `rightThird` (`left: 1180px`, `width: 644px`) open for Liquid Glass cards and keep `bottomDock` (`bottom 212px`) completely clear of forearms.

### Hand-Rest Framing Zone

Rest both forearms lightly along the front edge of the dark leather desk blotter, palms unclasped and angled slightly inward near center-left (`x = 550` to `950px`). Keep gestures compact inside the rectangular window between your mid-sternum and the cotton filings on the desk. Never lift fingers over the tie knot, brush past your chin, slap the leather blotter (which spikes low-end rumble on `A1`), or reach into the `rightThird` graphic overlay column.

## Phase 7 (`T-10:00` to `T-05:00`): Boom Mic Geometry and 20-Second Acoustic Test

Suspend the supercardioid small-diaphragm condenser (`Sennheiser MKH 50` or `Oktava MK-012` class) on a shockmount `28` to `35 cm` above your forehead, angled downward `45 deg` toward your sternum. Aiming above the nose line lets high-velocity `/p/`, `/b/`, and `/t/` bursts drop cleanly below the diaphragm while capturing warm chest resonance at `48,000 Hz / 24-bit` WAV.

Set preamp gain so normal conversational peaks hit `-12` to `-9 dBFS` with maximum angry-data emphasis peaking below `-6 dBFS`, keeping full headroom for two-pass `loudnorm` to `-14.0 LUFS` and `-1.0 dBTP`. Record a `20`-second calibration read using two numeric lines from the current episode, then play it back through closed-back studio headphones listening specifically for cotton collar rub against the neck, chair squeaks, `6.5 kHz` sibilance spikes, and `240 Hz` desk honk.

## Phase 8 (`T-05:00` to `T-00:00`): Final Sip, Clean Plate, and Live Roll

At `T-05:00`, take two small sips of room-temperature water (`50 ml`) without clearing your throat harshly, run one mirror check on top button, tie dimple, and collar balance, and read the opening hook sentence silently in your head at `150 WPM`.

At `T-02:00`, press record on both the camera and audio recorder, then step completely out of frame while silent. Hold an empty locked-tripod room frame for `10` full seconds (`5.0` seconds minimum required by `room_object_swap.py extract-plate`) so the editorial pipeline gets a clean room plate for alpha masking and background graphics. Walk quietly back to the desk at `T-01:40`, sit carefully without bumping the mic stand or leather blotter, settle your cuffs once, and hold still for `5` seconds. Over the final `30` seconds, take three silent `4`-second nasal belly breaths, blink once deliberately at `T-00:05`, lock your eyes square into the `35mm` lens center behind the prompter glass, hold `2.0` full seconds of clean room silence, and begin Line 1 at `T-00:00`.

## Solo Retakes Psychology and Clean Edit-Seam Rule

Solo shoots punish emotional reactions far harder than verbal slips. Every groan, eye-roll, sigh, finger snap, or chair shift contaminates posture continuity and floods your vocal fold tension with adrenaline. Follow four strict recovery mechanics whenever a word or figure trips:

1. Freeze your torso, chin tilt, and lens gaze on the exact frame where you stumble. Stay completely still. Keep your hands settled cleanly on the blotter until the next sentence starts.
2. Hold `1.5` full seconds of complete silence before breathing in. That `1.5`-second gap sits comfortably past the `0.65`-second / `-34.0 dB` silence threshold inside `prep-aroll.mjs`, giving the automatic silence trimmer clean room to carve out the blown take while leaving `120 ms` of intact natural breath padding on the good take.
3. Back up to the start of the previous complete sentence and skip mid-clause patches. Matching `150 WPM` cadence across a whole sentence lets DaVinci Resolve cut invisibly behind B-roll or a `100% -> 112%` punch-in.
4. Cap consecutive attempts on any stubborn line at three tries. If attempt three misses, turn the teleprompter scroll down one notch, read the line once slowly at `110 WPM` purely to train tongue placement, pause `2.0` silent seconds, and take attempt four cleanly at `150 WPM` before moving forward.
