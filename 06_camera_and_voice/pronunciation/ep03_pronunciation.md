## Persian Phonetic Legend

1. `ˈ` precedes each primary stressed syllable, and `·` marks spoken syllable breaks.
2. `و(W)` denotes rounded-lip `/w/` with zero contact between teeth and bottom lip.
3. `و(V)` denotes labiodental `/v/` where upper teeth lightly touch the bottom lip during vocal vibration.
4. `ث(TH-بی‌صدا)` denotes unvoiced `/θ/` formed with the tongue tip resting gently between front teeth.
5. `ذ(TH-صدادار)` denotes voiced `/ð/` formed in the exact same teeth-tongue stance while voicing the throat.
6. `ژ` marks `/ʒ/` as heard inside `Treasury` or `measure`.
