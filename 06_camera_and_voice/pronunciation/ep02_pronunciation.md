## Persian Phonetic Legend

1. `ˈ` marks the primary stressed syllable directly ahead, and `·` marks syllable splits.
2. `و(W)` signals rounded lips (`/w/`) with zero tooth contact.
3. `و(V)` signals top teeth touching bottom lip (`/v/`) with steady voice vibration.
4. `ث(TH-بی‌صدا)` marks unvoiced `/θ/` with tongue tip lightly between front teeth.
5. `ذ(TH-صدادار)` marks voiced `/ð/` in that same dental position with vocal cord hum.
6. `ژ` marks `/ʒ/` as in `Treasury`.

## Native Persian-to-English Interference Traps in Episode 02

### Trap 1: The /w/ Versus /v/ Collapse
Watch adjacent `/w/` and `/v/` pairs throughout Episode 02. Rounded `/w/` governs `withholding` (Line 14), `week` (Lines 16, 146), `wire` and `wires` (Lines 72, 74, 146), `web` (Lines 74, 150), `wallet` (Line 92), `Bretton Woods` (Line 134), `Fedwire` (Line 156), and `water` (Line 164).
